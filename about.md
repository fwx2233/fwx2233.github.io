I'm Wenxuan Fu, a PhD student with a bachelor's degree in Information Security from Nankai University, where I studied from 2019 to 2023.

My research interests include discovering vulnerabilities in Internet of Things (IoT) systems and exploring how large language models (LLMs) and AI agents can support security research.

On this blog, I share paper reading notes, research reflections, and lessons from hands-on experiments.

You can reach me at [fuwenxuan@mail.nankai.edu.cn](mailto:fuwenxuan@mail.nankai.edu.cn).
